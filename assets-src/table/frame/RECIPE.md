# Recipes: the frame, the marquee and the loader

Everything of these three parts is geometry made in code, except one picture.

## marquee-skyline.webp (colour, the picture behind the marquee's title)

Made by: the workspace's image tool with its default model, called once on 2026-11-25. The tool's answer does not name the model (UNKNOWN); the tool's guide STATES the default is its GPT Image model. Then `node assets-src/table/frame/make.mjs` (sharp, the version in `package.json`), which cuts one band out of the take and writes both sets.
From: `assets-src/table/frame/marquee-reference.jpg` (the marquee cut out of `references/approved/table-wide.png`, passed as the reference picture) and `assets-src/table/frame/marquee-skyline-take-1.png` (the kept take, 1672 by 941)
Prompt or command: "Image 1 is a reference: the lit panel at the top of a tabletop machine, showing a city skyline at dusk under a glowing sunset sky. Keep from it: the mood, the palette (deep blue-violet clouds at the top and sides, a warm orange and gold glow low at the centre of the horizon), and the character of the skyline (a dense downtown of many slim towers in dark silhouette with tiny warm lit windows, tallest towers left of centre and right of centre, lower in the middle where the glow is). Change: make only the flat artwork itself, as a photorealistic cinematic photograph of a real city at dusk, with no frame, no machine, no display, and with every letter and number removed. Composition: an extremely wide panorama, 16:9 frame. The skyline runs unbroken from the left edge to the right edge as a thin horizontal band across the exact middle of the frame, towers small, the tallest reaching no higher than a sixth of the frame height above the horizon. Above it a broad open sky of layered clouds lit from below by the sunset, calm and uncluttered in the centre. Below the horizon a dark still bay reflecting a little of the glow, fading to near-black at the bottom. Fine detail, soft atmospheric haze, natural film grain, no lens flare. Strictly no text, no letters, no numbers, no signs, no logos, no watermarks, no people, no faces, no hands, no birds, no animals, no aircraft, no vehicles." Aspect 16:9. Then `node assets-src/table/frame/make.mjs`: the band left 1, top 331, 1670 by 528 of the take, resized to 1296 by 336 (web, `public/assets/table/frame/`) and to 1670 by 433 (full, `assets-full/table/frame/`).
Takes: 1 made, take 1 kept
Why this one: the skyline runs edge to edge with the glow in the middle as in the approved marquee, and looked at whole and in two halves at full size it holds no letter, number, sign, person or animal
Date: 2026-11-25

The picture fills the whole face of the board; the bar, the display strip and the sill of the housing cover its lower part, so the water under the skyline is never seen. Until the picture is loaded, and wherever the asset set lacks it, the face is the skyline drawn by rule in `src/table/real/frame/skyline.ts`.
