# Step 2: the slot machine and the crank

Owner: step2-slot. Branch `agent/step2-slot`. Files: `src/pages/step2/real/slot*.ts`, `crank*.ts`, `tests/step2-slot*.test.ts`, `assets-src/step2-slot/`.

## What the approved images show that a quick look misses

Read from `references/approved/step2-crank.png` and `machines.png` at full size, in crops of the crank, the meter and the cabinet.

1. Highlight. On the crank's bezel and hub the light is not a spot: it is a narrow bow tie through the centre, the mark of a face turned on a lathe. On the grip it is a soft band along the upper left long edge. On the reels it is a broad band across the middle that falls to dark grey toward the top and the bottom of the window, which is what makes them drums and not cards.
2. Bevel. The bezel's rim is a polished chamfer about 3 px wide on a 55 px radius, brighter than the face. The hub's chamfer is about 2 px. The arm's edges are under 1.5 px and only the upper left one is bright. The cabinet's frame is about 26 px wide and has two steps: a flat brushed outer face, then a slope down to the glass.
3. Gap. The hub sits in a dark ring about 2 px wide, near 0x2a, never black. The screws are dark crosses in countersunk heads. Between the reels the gap is about 10 px of near black brown, and the window is dark for 6 to 8 px inside the frame before the drum begins.
4. Edge. An unlit meter pill is clear glass: a light line about 2 px wide all round its rim and a darker core, standing on a short shadow to the lower right. A lit pill is yellow white at its centre line and falls to orange at the rim, and it spills orange light about 15 px over the tray. The bezel's lower right rim picks up orange from the grip.
5. The lever. Its arm is a round polished rod with one bent elbow coming out of a block on the cabinet's side, not a flat bar. The knob is a matt orange ball with a broad soft highlight to the upper left, and its shadow is the longest and softest in the picture. An orange hairline crosses the three reels at their middle, with a small pin in each gap.

## Decisions

1. Kit file `slot-kit.ts` holds what both parts use: `lathe`, `loft`, `fuse`, `picture`, `normalsFrom`, `rise`.
2. A machine rises by its height: every object is built at its full height and its z is scaled by the share of the height the page sends. Below 2% it is hidden and throws nothing.
3. The crank's arm is wider at the hub than the page's capsule (21 against 16 px) and tapers to 16 at the grip, because a forged crank arm has an eye round its shaft. Its length and its place are the page's.
4. Meter segments are built 12 px tall for the page's 20 (lit: the page's 26 of 20, so 15.6). At 20 px a pill throws a shadow longer than the gap to its neighbour and the row reads as a fence, and the approved image shows shallow glass. The pill follows the page's height by share, because on the `machines` sheet the meter stands on the page with its floor at the tray's depth and a pill of a fixed low height was buried. The floor and the footprint are the page's.
5. The meter's light: at most 4 lamps, never a shadow lamp. The lit segments are split in up to 4 groups and the middle segment of each group gives the group's light, with the power of all its segments. Ten lit segments cost 4 of the 6 lamps.
6. The cabinet's frame stands 12 px higher than the page says (top at 50 and not 38). The page puts the legends at 44, above its own cabinet, and a window with real glass needs the frame and the glass above what is read through it: drum crest at 42, pay line at 42.75, legend at 44 (the type maker's, untouched), glass at 46.2, frame at 50. Asked of the dial maker: nothing has to change for this to work. If the cursor is ever seen to cut the frame, `slot-cabinet` should go to `TOP + 20`.
7. `slot-window` and `slot-reel-0..2` answer `''`: the cabinet's object draws the well, the drums, the pay line and the glass. `coin-N` answers `''`: the payout's object draws all coins as one instanced mesh, read from `ctx.list` by the payout's own tag, so the sheet's coins and the page's never mix.
8. The legends stay with the type maker, flat at 44. The drum under them has a radius of 62 px, so a legend 31 px off the middle floats 8 px above the drum. Under glass and seen from above it is not seen as floating.
9. The drums are lit as things in a deep well and not by the key: their shade toward the top and the bottom is painted in vertex colours, their normals are flattened to 40% up the page, and they throw no shadow (they take the frame's). With true normals the key at its low angle left the lower third black with a slanted edge.
10. The lever. The page moves the knob 96 px down toward a fixed pivot and keeps its height, which no rigid lever can do. It is built as a rod that slides through a swivelling barrel in the mount: the rod runs from the barrel (height 28) to under the ball (height 52) and is as long as the page's capsule needs. Seen from above it reads as a lever swinging toward the eye.
11. The knob is a ball 44 px across pressed to 28 px high: the page's heights, so the cursor that holds it stays on top of it.
12. A coin tumbles by its own numbers: the lean is `sin(1.3 rot + 2.4 n + 0.045 z)` times 1.05 rad, times how far it is off the page (full at 14 px). On the page it lies flat. A leaning coin is lifted so its low edge never goes under the height the page gives.
13. The glass is drawn after the type (`userData.order` 800000, the sheen 800001), below the overlays and the cursor.
14. Gold for the coins. It is the one colour outside the page's grey, white and orange, and it is on screen only for the two seconds of the payout.
15. An unlit segment is see-through: its map's alpha is 0.2 in the core and up to 0.9 on the bright line of the rim, so the tray shows through it. It still throws its shadow. A lit segment is opaque and gives its own light (emissive 1.0 through a painted map: pale orange core, deeper ring, bright rim line), with a weak clear coat so the lamp above it leaves no hot spot.
16. The pay line has almost no glow of its own (0.06 of the shared orange). At 0.45 the bloom of the line bled over the legends and they read as struck through. MEASURED: `npm run still -- --page step2 --beat step2.slot --at 0.8 --format hd --tier draft`, crop 1060,600,1180,650, the digits cover the line.
17. The bezel's and the hub's faces use a clone of the shared turned aluminium with weaker rings (normal scale 0.022) and the shared material's own shader hook. The arm has its own brushed bar material (clone of the shared aluminium, darker, brushing along the arm).
18. The crank's arm and grip take their size from the shape (`w`, `h`) and from `CRANK` of the page, so they follow the dial maker's change (grip 84 by 40, arm 24 wide) with no edit here. The cabinet's full height is read from the page's own layout (`slot-cabinet` height minus floor) so the frame keeps its 58 px whether the page says 46 or 58.
19. Lamp power 0.12 for each lit segment, reach 170 px, 14 px above the pill.
20. The payout's mouth is 3 px deep (floor at 8.5 above the tray floor) and not 10. On the `machines` sheet the machine stands on the page with its floor 8 px under it, and a deeper mouth showed the page's ground through its floor.
21. The frame is darker and less brushed than the shared aluminium (0x6a6e72, roughness 0.5, brushing 0.07) after the approved image's bead blasted frame. The drums fall to dark faster toward the top and the bottom (share of light = 0.05 + 0.95 cos^3.6).
22. The lever's mount: a block in the frame's metal with two screw heads, and a chrome barrel with a collar at each end.
23. The grip is a bar of soft plastic and not a sausage: a squared cross section (superellipse of power 3.2) so its top is flat, round ends, 10% fuller at its free end, with a collar 5% thinner and a seam 9 px from the arm's end. It fills the page's box and height.
24. The bezel's and the hub's turned faces carry a bow tie in vertex colour (0.8 to 1.75 times the metal's colour, along the axis toward the key light). The shared metal's anisotropy gives the shape of the highlight but under the studio's light it was too flat to read as a lathe's face.
25. The drums carry no mark of their own, as in the approved image. `v[0]` of `slot-reel-N` is received and not used: a blank drum that turns looks the same, and the legends that pass over it are the page's.
26. Looks made after 01:15 UTC show my branch with the dial maker's branch laid over it (`src/pages/step2/index.ts`, `layout.ts`, `real/dial.ts`, `real/rows.ts` from `origin/agent/step2-dial`, checked out for the render and put back), because the sizes agreed with it are not on `main` yet.
27. The pay line is a wire in front of the legends and no longer under them: 0.6 px thick at 45.1, between the legends (44.03) and the glass (46.2), as in the approved image where the hairline crosses the digits. Under the legends it showed through them at the review tier and not at the draft tier (seen with the sheen on, not with it off; the cause is not in my files and I did not find it), so the same picture could not be promised at every tier. This replaces the heights of decision 6 (line at 42.75) and the claim of decision 16 that the digits cover the line. MEASURED: `npm run still -- --page step2 --beat step2.slot --at 0.3 --format hd --tier review`, crop 1090,540,1450,680, one unbroken hairline over the digits.
28. The window's glass veils less: opacity 0.12 (was 0.2), reflection 1.5 (was 2.2), sheen 0.1 at the top and 0.045 in the streak. The sheen is added in linear light, and 0.06 turned the black gaps between the drums into a mid grey.
29. An unlit segment is tinted glass, a little darker than the liner (0.84), with its bright rim line only on the side the light comes from and a dark line all round. A white ring all round read as white plastic. The pills stand 6.5 px high and not 12, so their shadows are shorter (a glass capsule with a long dark shadow read as opaque). The tint was then deepened to 0.7 so the capsules keep a body on the white page of the sheet. Lit core is paler, a cream (green up to 0.88, blue up to 0.74) and the lamp is 0.2 for each lit segment (was 0.12).
30. The crank's arm reads as a forging: its eye stands 3.4 px outside the hub's footprint (radius 25.4), the bar leaves the eye 38 px wide and tapers to 24 at the grip, and the cap on the hub is smaller (radius 16.5, was 22) so the eye shows as a ring round it, as in the approved image. The bar is lighter (0x989da2) and the bow tie on the turned faces brighter (1.05 to 2.2 times the metal's colour). Lamp 0.16 for each lit segment. The orange mark the lamps leave on the tray's glossy rim is the rim's reflection of them; the approved image has the same tint on its rim.
31. A meter segment stands on the floor of its tray when the list has a tray for it (`volume-tray`), and on the page (z 0) when it has none. On the `machines` sheet the shapes keep the tray's floor (-8) but there is no tray, so a pill 6.5 px high was wholly under the page: the look `machines-0150` shows only the four lit segments and its caption is wrong. MEASURED after the change: `npm run still -- --sheet machines --format hd --tier review`, all ten segments stand on the page with their shadows. The lamp's height follows the same rule.
