# The models of Tier 1, The Street

Owner: the modeller of the street. Branch `agent/street`. Files: `src/table/real/models/street/`, `tests/table-street.test.ts`, this file.

## What the approved images show that a quick look misses

1. The firehouse is two materials, not one: a pale stone ground storey under red brick, with a stone frame round the door, and its roof is a grey deck inside a parapet with small units on it, not a red lid.
2. Both beacons are ribbed domes on a chromed ring, and the roof round each is washed in its colour: the light lands on things.
3. The bank is lit from inside: the warm light is behind the columns, on the wall and the doors, and the columns stand dark against it on a base of real steps.
4. The houses stand on a kerbed strip of their own with grass on it, and no two roofs have the same colour or the same shape.
5. The road signs are plates that stand clear of the floor on the sling posts, with a bright wire edge and screw heads at the posts, and the rubber shows under them.

## What is built

All of it is code: `geo.ts` (a builder of triangles with a place, a normal, a colour and a `face` attribute), `mats.ts` (one painted material whose fragment shader lays brick courses with mortar over them, burnt bricks, stone blocks, board marked concrete, shingles, clapboard, stucco, gloss paint, honeycomb sheeting and roofing felt from the `face` attribute, with stains, streaks under ledges and a dark foot on upright faces; one metal; glowing surfaces; clear beacon lenses), `kit.ts` (walls with holes, windows with reveal, frame, glazing bars, sill, lintel and stone surround, parapets, beacons, sign boards), and one file for each model. There are no asset files: 0 MB in every asset set.

- `model-firehouse`: base plate, a pale stone ground storey under fine red brick, a stone base course, a string course, a cornice and long and short stone quoins at the corners of the upper storey, a parapet with a stone cap round a felt deck, a hose tower with louvres and a pyramid roof, roof hatch, cooling unit, two vents, lit windows in stone surrounds, a brass pole behind one of them, the bay painted oxide red inside with hose racks, a door of four panels with a row of small panes, a round cornered stone frame with a keystone and two yellow guard posts, the beacon, the sign board.
- `model-police`: blue painted brick on a stone plinth, a recessed entrance with four pale steps to double doors, a stone frame with a small pediment and a round lamp over the doors, barred ground windows, lit upper windows, parapet roof with a stair house, a radio mast, a cooling unit, two vents, the beacon, the sign board.
- `model-bank`: three steps, four stout fluted columns with moulded bases and capitals on square plinths, an entablature with dentils, an attic and a flat felt roof inside a parapet as in the approved picture, tall bronze doors with panels and a lit transom, a night deposit slot, a lamp in the porch ceiling, three tall lit windows on the side that faces the table, the sign board.
- `model-garage`: a round concrete frame outside the clear spiral. Deck edges under each turn with a slimmer rail beam half way between, a deeper rim beam with a steel coping, eight ribbed piers with a cap, a strip lamp under each deck edge in every bay, a cast joint in each deck edge. Open under 1.3 balls. Carried by two steel stanchions that stand on the top of the right wall, with an arm to the pier at every deck, and tied to the police front by two steel angles.
- `model-houses`: six houses on two kerbed, paved strips with a band of grass: plaster, brick and painted plaster, gable fronts with a lit gable window and two gables the other way, different heights, a door with a step and a path, lit windows, a chimney, a mailbox on a post, shrubs.
- `model-moms`: a clapboard house on a stone footing with a dark red roof, a porch with four posts, rails and a lean-to roof, a porch light, a flower box, a mat with nothing on it, gable windows, a mailbox, the sign board on two posts from the porch roof. The cellar hatch: a concrete frame round the mouth, two doors of painted steel tread plate with ribs, hinges and a ring pull, and a lit sleeve inside the mouth.
- `model-road-signs`: two pressed plates on the sling posts at 46 px, yellow honeycomb sheeting, bold black chevrons that point inward, a black line inside a rolled edge, a rivet at each post and one in the middle, grime along the lower edge. Each plate hops when its sling is struck.
- Street things on the two shoulders, where no ball can come: four street lamps, two hydrants, a bus shelter with a lit blank panel, two parked toy cars in dark paint.

## Decisions

- No picture files: every surface is geometry and a procedural surface in the shader, so there is no letter or number anywhere and nothing to load.
- The seven parts share one build for the zone (`streetOf`), and each part keeps the meshes that are its own, so that the static geometry of all seven is one mesh for each material. Colour pass: firehouse 5 (painted static of all models, door, door frame, red reflector, red lens), police 2 (blue reflector, blue lens), bank 1 (metal static of all models), garage 1 (lit surfaces of all models), houses 1 (screws, instanced, `fine`), Mom's 2 (cellar doors instanced twice, cellar light), road signs 2. Total 14.
- The garage, after the Builder's layout of 46e4bc6: it is the frame outside the trough. Its radius comes from `SPIRAL.frame` (146.4 px outer face, 137.3 px inner face). Every beam, pier, rail and lamp is tested against the tube of the ball before it is built (`tube`, `inTube` in `garage.ts`): points along `rail.highway` and `rail.spiral` a twelfth of a ball apart, 60 px to the side (the ball's radius and a tenth of `table.minClearanceBalls`), 120 px over the path, 13 px under it, plus a margin of 3 px, and 10 px more round the highway for its wires. What falls inside is left out: that opens the rim where the highway comes in and the lowest deck edge where the tail leaves. A test holds every vertex outside the trough and outside the tube.
- No pier reaches the floor. The Builder named the sling post, the lane post, the right wall and the police corner as the places to stand on: I chose the right wall, since a stanchion on the wall's top narrows no lane at all. Under 1.3 balls the garage has only those two stanchions, which stand outside `COLS.right`. The height of the wall's top (60 px) is the frame owner's `WALL.height` in `frame/tier.ts`, typed again in `garage.ts` because that file exports nothing: if the wall changes, `WALL_TOP` must follow.
- No core: play's clear newel (radius 9) stands in the middle.
- Sign boards stand at the place, size and turn of each `sign.<model id>` text shape, read from the draw list, on two brackets from the roof. They draw no letter.
- The firehouse door stands raised at rest (0.86 of its travel, its lowest panel in the top of the opening), since the approved picture shows the bay open and dark. It runs right up in 0.18 s as a ball is caught, stays up while the ball is held and for 1.4 s after it is fired out, with the frame shaking, then comes back. The window with the brass pole is the second from the left, clear of the door's travel.
- The firehouse has a pale stone ground storey under red brick, as the approved picture has, with the stone again in the bands, the quoins of the upper storey and the door frame. For twenty minutes it was brick on both storeys, which was a misreading of a small comparison; the crop of the approved picture settled it. Bricks are 6 by 2.5 px, finer than the first version, since the approved model shows about fifty across the front.
- The bank has a flat roof inside a parapet with the sign on its front edge, as the approved picture has; the first version had a pediment.
- The cellar doors are painted steel tread plate, not boards: a brown rectangle was what the critics look for first. They lie on a frame that is 3.5 px high where the ball comes in and 8 px at the far end, hinged on the long sides, and open to 86 degrees so that a ball passes between them.
- The beacons are clear tinted domes (opacity 0.9) over a grey mirror and a bright bulb. The dome's glow is 0.18 + 0.6 times the level of its group and the bulb's 0.3 + 3 times, so the dome is seen as red or blue plastic when the lamp is out.
- The paint of the garage and the bank is set for the lights owner's lower window sources (garage source at 110 px with power 0.9, bank's two at 70 px with power 0.3 and 0.2).
- The plate of a road sign is the post triangle grown by 6 px with corners of 9 px, as agreed with the hardware owner.
- Street things are placed from the boxes of Mom's house and the rows of houses, on the shoulders behind `shoulder.edge` and `inlane.floor`, where the layout has no opening and no rail.

## Agreed with other owners

- Hardware: sling posts end in a flat shoulder 46 px over the floor, radius 7 px; the plate's underside is at 46 px and the rivets through it are mine; the band shows under it. The scoop mouths are theirs, flush in the floor, radius 0.58 balls, nothing over 6 px: the firehouse door and the cellar doors over them are mine.
- Play: the clear trough has walls 4 thick and 33 tall, a lip out to 136.5 px from the middle, a newel of radius 9, and tabs every 150 px of path out to the frame. I told play that my inner face is at 137.3 px, not 146.4, so the tabs end there; that I have nothing inside 137.3 px and nothing under 1.3 balls; and where the frame is left open.
- Lights: my lit surfaces carry `userData.lightGroup` (`street.red`, `street.blue`, `street.windows`) and take their level from `levels()`. The lights owner lowered the window sources at the garage and the bank after I showed that they burnt the paint out, and I set the paint back to its true tones. Beacons have power 1.1 and reach 5.2 balls. My four street lamps are sources in `street.windows`. There is no source in the open cellar: all ten channels are in use.
- Ball: nothing of mine lies where the ball rolls in the Street; plinths and strips are 3 px thick inside the model boxes.

## Known faults

- From straight above (`tier-street`) the garage's rim, 5.6 balls up, stands in front of the right half of the police sign. The frame is where the layout puts it; the sign is the type owner's. Reported to the Builder.
