## Notes on reading

- The images are made through the image tool of Perplexity Computer, so the direct Google customer is Perplexity, not this project. Whether Perplexity calls the Gemini API or Vertex AI is UNKNOWN, so both sets of terms are quoted.

## (a) Who owns the output

- "Some of our Services allow you to generate original content. Google won't claim ownership over that content. You acknowledge that Google may generate the same or similar content for others and that we reserve all rights to do so."
  Source: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms

- "“Generated Output” means the data or content generated by a Generative AI Service prompted by Customer Data. Generated Output is Customer Data. As between Customer and Google, Google does not assert any ownership rights in any new intellectual property created in the Generated Output."
  Source: https://cloud.google.com/terms/service-terms

## (b) Commercial use

- "Use of Google AI Studio and Gemini API is for developers building with Google AI models for professional or business purposes, not for consumer use."
  Source: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms

- No sentence was found that says in so many words that generated content may be used commercially. The sentence above is about who the service is for.

## (c) Restrictions that touch a published video or a public website

- "As required by the API Terms, you'll comply with applicable law in using generated content, which may require the provision of attribution to your users when returned as part of an API call. Use discretion before relying on generated content, including code. You're responsible for your use of generated content, and for the use of that content by anyone you share it with."
  Source: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms

- "Use discretion before relying on, publishing, or otherwise using content provided by the Services."
  Source: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms

- "Do not engage in misinformation, misrepresentation, or misleading activities. This includes:"
  Source: https://policies.google.com/terms/generative-ai/use-policy

- "Misrepresenting the provenance of generated content by claiming it was created solely by a human, in order to deceive."
  Source: https://policies.google.com/terms/generative-ai/use-policy

## (d) Watermark, provenance mark, attribution

- "All generated images include a SynthID watermark."
  Source: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/image-generation

- No sentence was found in the Gemini API Additional Terms that names SynthID or any watermark. The SynthID statement is on the documentation page only.

## Conclusion

- Ownership of outputs: STATED. "Google won't claim ownership over that content." and, for Google Cloud, "Google does not assert any ownership rights in any new intellectual property created in the Generated Output." The terms say Google does not claim it. They do not say in so many words that the user owns it.
- Published video: not clearly allowed. UNKNOWN. The terms speak of "publishing, or otherwise using" content with discretion, and make the user responsible for use, but no quoted sentence grants commercial use or publication of image output. No quoted sentence forbids it.
- Public website: not clearly allowed. UNKNOWN. Same reason as for video.
- Watermark or attribution duty: STATED in part. The documentation says "All generated images include a SynthID watermark." The terms say applicable law "may require the provision of attribution to your users when returned as part of an API call", and the Prohibited Use Policy forbids "Misrepresenting the provenance of generated content by claiming it was created solely by a human, in order to deceive." A duty to show a visible credit on a website or in a video is UNKNOWN.
