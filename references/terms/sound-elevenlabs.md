# ElevenLabs terms, as read for this build

Date read: 2026-11-25

Scope: narrator speech made with ElevenLabs text to speech in a saved Voice Design voice, and sound effects made with the ElevenLabs sound effects endpoint, on the Creator plan paid monthly. Only elevenlabs.io pages were used. Every quote below was checked word for word against the live page on the date read. Curly quotation marks inside a quote are the source page's own.

Which Terms of Service apply: the page read is the non-EEA version. It says it applies "if you reside outside of the European Economic Area (EEA), Switzerland, or UK". A separate EEA version exists at https://elevenlabs.io/terms-of-use-eu and was not read.

## Conclusions

Each line says only what the quotes above support. STATED means the page says it in so many words. UNKNOWN means the pages read do not clearly say.

1. Publishing inside a film and inside a website, Creator plan. One line: STATED for sound effects inside a film (allowed on a paid plan, when combined with other material). UNKNOWN for speech inside a film and for both kinds of audio inside a website (only general commercial use wording). Detail:
   - Sound effects inside a film: STATED, allowed. "You may use, reproduce, modify, distribute, publicly perform, and display Output, including by incorporating it into films, trailers, advertising, video, games, applications, and other productions", and "for commercial purposes only where your plan permits commercial use". Condition, also stated: "only in respect of Output combined with other material". Source: https://elevenlabs.io/creative-terms
   - Speech (text to speech in a saved Voice Design voice) inside a film: UNKNOWN in the terms. The Terms of Service do not name films for speech. They state only the general rule: a "Paid User" "may use the Services for commercial purposes", downloaded Output may be used "outside of the Services", and "you retain all rights in and to your Output". Source: https://elevenlabs.io/terms-of-use . The Text to Speech product page is not consistent: one served version lists "YouTube videos, podcasts, advertisements, audiobooks, films, games, and apps", the other lists "YouTube videos, podcasts, ads, audiobooks, games, and apps" with no films. Source: https://elevenlabs.io/text-to-speech
   - Inside a website, both kinds of audio: UNKNOWN in so many words. No page read uses the word "website" for where output may be published. The nearest wording is "applications, and other productions" for sound effects and "apps" for speech. For sound effects on a website the stated limits still apply: "combined with other material", and not "available as isolated audio files". Source: https://elevenlabs.io/creative-terms
   - Creator plan has a commercial licence: STATED. Starter lists "Commercial License" and Creator is "Everything in Starter, plus". Source: https://elevenlabs.io/pricing . "All paid plans include a commercial license, provided you’re not using Beta Services." Source: https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/legal/can-i-publish-the-content-i-generate-on-the-platform
   - Open flag for the saved Voice Design voice: UNKNOWN. A help article says "Voice Design is still experimental". Beta Services are those "designated as alpha, beta, pilot, preview, or similar designation" and may not be used "for any commercial purposes or in any production environment". No page read says whether Voice Design counts as a Beta Service. Sources: https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/product/voices/voice-design/what-is-voice-design and https://elevenlabs.io/bsa

2. Keeping the generated audio as separate, individually downloadable files in a public code repository. One line: STATED for sound effects (not allowed as isolated audio files or as a collection of sounds). UNKNOWN for speech files. Detail:
   - Sound effects: STATED, not allowed as separate files. "you may not sell, resell, license, sublicense, distribute, or otherwise commercially exploit Output, or any part of it, on a standalone basis, or make Output available as isolated audio files or samples, or as part of any sound, sample, effects, or music library or other collection of sounds." The only stated exceptions are Community Sharing and "any other use ElevenLabs expressly permits in writing". The terms do not name code repositories or GitHub; the rule is about isolated audio files in general. Source: https://elevenlabs.io/creative-terms
   - Speech files: UNKNOWN. No page read has a rule about standalone or isolated speech files, or about redistribution of speech output. What is stated is general: "you retain all rights in and to your Output" and downloaded Output may be used "outside of the Services" (Source: https://elevenlabs.io/terms-of-use ), and it is prohibited to make "Output available to your own end users or other third parties on terms more permissive than those that apply to you" or to include "Output in a dataset used for" AI training (Source: https://elevenlabs.io/use-policy ). Whether a public repository under an open licence meets or breaks those two rules is not said.

3. A video posted by a company on social media. One line: STATED that a paid plan may be used for commercial purposes, that sound effects may go into advertising and social media content, and that the free plan must attribute. UNKNOWN in so many words whether speech on a paid plan needs attribution. Detail:
   - Commercial use on a paid plan: STATED, allowed. "if you access or use our Services through a paid subscription plan (such a user, a “Paid User”), you may use the Services for commercial purposes". Source: https://elevenlabs.io/terms-of-use . Use for an entity is covered by Section 1(b), which makes "that person or entity" also bound and responsible. Source: https://elevenlabs.io/terms-of-use
   - Sound effects in social media and advertising: STATED, allowed on a paid plan. Exhibit 4 names "advertising" and "video". Source: https://elevenlabs.io/creative-terms . The product page says "including YouTube videos, social media content, and even advertising". Source: https://elevenlabs.io/sound-effects
   - Speech in advertising: STATED on the product page only, in both served versions ("advertisements" in one, "ads" in the other). Source: https://elevenlabs.io/text-to-speech
   - Attribution on the free plan: STATED, required. "you must attribute it to ElevenLabs by including “elevenlabs.io” or “11.ai” in the title". Source: https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/legal/can-i-publish-the-content-i-generate-on-the-platform
   - Attribution on a paid plan: STATED for sound effects, not required ("Our paid plans do not require attribution". Source: https://elevenlabs.io/sound-effects/commercial ). UNKNOWN in so many words for speech: the help article says "Attribution requirement is subject to the agreement you have with ElevenLabs", the Terms of Service contain no attribution clause, and the Text to Speech page states the duty for the free plan only. Sources: https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/legal/can-i-publish-the-content-i-generate-on-the-platform and https://elevenlabs.io/terms-of-use
   - Advertising limits that are stated: no "deceptive advertisements" and no "falsely implying endorsement, sponsorship, or affiliation"; no "Political campaigning without our prior written consent"; no use of the ElevenLabs "name, trademarks, hidden text, or branding" without written consent; disclose AI "where required by law or where failing to do so would materially mislead". Source: https://elevenlabs.io/use-policy
