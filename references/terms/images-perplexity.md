## Conclusion

- Ownership of outputs: STATED for Perplexity Enterprise. "Customer (i) retains all ownership rights in Input and (ii) owns all Output. Perplexity asserts no ownership rights in any Output and, to the extent it obtains any such rights by operation of law or otherwise, hereby assigns such rights to Customer." UNKNOWN for the consumer Terms, which do not say the user owns Output.
- Published video: not clearly allowed. UNKNOWN. Enterprise gives ownership of Output, but no quoted sentence in either agreement names commercial use or publication, the consumer Terms carry a restriction on publishing "any information contained on, or obtained from or through, the Services", and outputs "may also be subject to license and use restrictions set forth in a third-party LLM license".
- Public website: not clearly allowed. UNKNOWN. Same reason as for video.
- Watermark or attribution duty: STATED in part. "You may not misrepresent the source of any Output or the fact that it was generated by artificial intelligence." The Acceptable Use Policy forbids to "Remove watermarks, metadata or other indicia intended to identify outputs as artificially generated or manipulated". A duty to show a visible credit is UNKNOWN. Whether re-encoding an image as a texture, which can drop metadata, counts as removal is UNKNOWN.
