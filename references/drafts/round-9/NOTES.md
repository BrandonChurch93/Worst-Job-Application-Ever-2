How the Builder read it (logged as a decision, read back to him in Status 171): "like one" is LOOK 1, the walker. "Cannon foreign validations" are the red validation slabs the guns fire today: every gun fires bullets the visitor dodges. The new arenas start from BOSS 6 of round 7b (broken ground, pillars, glass), with the top of the page removed and only the boss's health bar there.
